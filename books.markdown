First, my two books so far:

- [Getting Started with PowerShell](https://www.packtpub.com/networking-and-servers/getting-started-powershell)
- [PowerShell TroubleShooting Guide](https://www.packtpub.com/networking-and-servers/powershell-troubleshooting-guide)

There are too many good books to list them all, but here are some favorites:

- [PowerShell In Action, 2nd Edition](https://www.amazon.com/Windows-PowerShell-Action-Second-Payette/dp/1935182137/ref=dp_ob_title_bk) by Bruce Payette
- [Windows PowerShell Cookbook (2nd edition)](https://www.amazon.com/Windows-PowerShell-Cookbook-Scripting-Microsofts/dp/0596801505) by Lee Holmes
- [Professional Windows PowerShell Programming](https://www.amazon.com/Professional-Windows-PowerShell-Programming-Providers/dp/0470173939/ref=sr_1_12?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1252807069&sr=8-12) by Arul Kumaravel, Jon White, Michael Naixin Li, and Scott Happell
- [Learn PowerShell in a Month of Lunches, 2nd Edition](https://www.amazon.com/Learn-Windows-PowerShell-Month-Lunches/dp/1617291080) by Don Jones and Jeffery Hicks
- [Learn PowerShell Toolmaking in a Month of Lunches](https://www.amazon.com/Learn-PowerShell-Toolmaking-Month-Lunches/dp/1617291161/) by Don Jones and Jeffery Hicks
